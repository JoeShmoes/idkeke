Privacy Policy

Transparency is core to our philosophy. Here's how we handle your data with the care and respect it deserves.
Last updated: February 15, 2026

At Nexaris, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you access the Nexaris web application, request early access, create an account, or use our services.

By using Nexaris, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1
Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

Personal Information

Information you voluntarily provide, including but not limited to:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Username
  • Account credentials
  • Contact information
  • Any data you submit within the platform (notes, tasks, files, content, workflows)

Usage & Technical Data

Information automatically collected when you use the web app:

  • IP address
  • Browser type
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Access times
  • Pages visited
  • Interaction data within the app
  • Performance and diagnostic data

This data helps us improve functionality, performance, and security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Provide access to the Nexaris platform
  • Operate and maintain the web application
  • Improve features, performance, and user experience
  • Communicate updates, system notifications, and important service information
  • Provide customer support
  • Ensure platform security and prevent abuse
  • Develop future features and integrations

We do not sell your personal data.

3. Data Storage & Processing

All user data is securely stored and processed through protected systems and infrastructure. Your content remains yours.

Nexaris does not access, analyze, or use your private workspace data except:

  • To provide core platform functionality
  • To ensure system stability and security
  • When legally required

4. Disclosure of Information

We may share information only in the following situations:

Legal Requirements

If required by law, court order, or legal process.

Security & Protection

To protect the rights, safety, property, and integrity of Nexaris, its users, and the public.

Service Providers

With trusted infrastructure and service providers who help operate the platform (hosting, analytics, security), under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Security

We use technical, administrative, and organizational security measures to protect user data, including:

  • Encrypted connections
  • Secure authentication systems
  • Access controls
  • Infrastructure-level protections

While no system can guarantee 100% security, Nexaris is built with security-first architecture and modern protection standards.

6. User Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Update your information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Control your account settings and permissions

You maintain ownership of your data at all times.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Nexaris evolves. Any changes will be posted within the platform or on the website. Continued use of the service means acceptance of the updated policy.

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding privacy or data protection, please contact us at:
